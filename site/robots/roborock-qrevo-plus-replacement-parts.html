What changes the fit on the Roborock Qrevo Plus

The full name is Qrevo Plus, also written Q Revo Plus. Plus is not Qrevo Pro. The main-brush cover is one of the official titles that prints Plus by name beside Pro, S, and MaxV.

That cover title reads Qrevo, Qrevo Pro, Qrevo Plus, Qrevo S, Qrevo MaxV, QV 35A, and QV 35S. Qrevo Curv and Qrevo Edge are left off the cover and off the main-brush title that includes Plus.

The Plus washable filter is the 2-piece whose title runs Qrevo S, Qrevo MaxV, Qrevo Pro, Qrevo Plus, Qrevo Curv Series, Qrevo Edge Series, QV Series, and Qrevo S5V. Curv and Edge on the filter title are not permission to buy their brushes.

A second filter, titled for Roborock Qrevo only, states that it is incompatible with other models in the Qrevo series. Qrevo Master, Qrevo Slim, and Saros Z70 have a third 2-piece of their own.

Plus mop cloths come from the 4-piece that also names S5V, original Qrevo, Pro, S, Master, MaxV, Slim, and CurvX. The 6-piece dock bag does not print the word Plus. It says Qrevo Series, and it also names S8 MaxV Ultra, S8 Max Ultra, Curv Series, Edge Series, Saros Series, and QV Series.
