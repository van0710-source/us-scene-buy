What changes the fit on the Dreame X40 Ultra

Dreame’s kit title for this robot is X40 Ultra/L40 Ultra. The compatibility line is X40 Ultra, L40 Ultra, and GoVac 508.

Counted contents are 1 main brush, 2 side brushes, 2 dust-collection bags, 2 dust-box filters, and 6 wash-free mop pads.

L40 Ultra Gen 2 is not on that list. Dreame’s Gen 2 kit is titled L40 Ultra Gen 2, names L40 Ultra Gen 2 and GoVac 405, and counts a rubber roller brush, 2 side brushes, 2 dust-box filters, 2 dust bags, and 6 mops.

L40s Ultra AE, L40s Ultra CE, X30 Ultra, and L10s Ultra Gen2 belong to the other accessory kit. That kit packs 3 wash-free mop pads, not these 6, and its compatibility line includes GoVac 400 and GoVac 500 rather than GoVac 508.
