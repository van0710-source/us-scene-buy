What changes the fit on the Dreame L10s Ultra Gen 2

The machine label has to include Gen 2. Dreame also writes that suffix as Gen2, without a space. Original L10s Ultra, with neither form, is a different kit page.

The official kit title leads with L40s Ultra AE, L40s Ultra CE, and L10s Ultra Gen2. The compatibility line names X30 Ultra, L40s Ultra AE, L40s Ultra CE, L10s Pro Ultra, L10s Ultra Gen2, D30 Ultra, D30 Ultra CE, GoVac 400, and GoVac 500.

Contents of that kit are 1 main brush, 2 side brushes, 2 dust-collection bags, 2 dust-box filters, and 3 wash-free mop pads.

The original L10s Ultra kit lists only L10s Ultra and packs 6 mop pads (3 pairs), 2 filters, and 2 dust bags. Its 3-pack dust-bag page says the bags are made for L10s Ultra and L20 Ultra, not for Gen 2. X40 Ultra and original L40 Ultra are on a third kit, with 6 wash-free mop pads and GoVac 508.
