What changes the fit on the Dreame L20 Ultra

The L20 Ultra accessory-kit page says the kit is made for Dreame Robot Vacuum L20 Ultra. Counted contents are 1 main roller brush, 2 side brushes, 2 filters, 6 mop pads (3 pairs), and 2 dust bags.

Dreame’s L20 Ultra dust-box filter page says the 2-pack is compatible with the L20 Ultra and recommends replacing that filter every 3 to 6 months. That sentence names L20 Ultra, not L10s Ultra.

Dust bags are the shared consumable. The 3-pack page says the bags are made specifically for L20 Ultra and L10s Ultra. The L20 kit page does not say the roller, side brushes, filters, or mop pads fit the L10s Ultra.

L40 Ultra is a different dock family. Its kit title is X40 Ultra/L40 Ultra, the compatibility line adds GoVac 508, and the kit counts 6 wash-free mop pads. L10s Ultra Gen 2 is on yet another kit, the one that leads with L40s Ultra AE and packs 3 wash-free mop pads.
