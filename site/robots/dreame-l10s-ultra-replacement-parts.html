What changes the fit on the Dreame L10s Ultra

Dreame’s L10s Ultra accessory-kit page lists one robot: L10s Ultra. The kit contains 1 main roller brush, 2 side brushes, 2 filters, 6 mop pads (3 pairs), and 2 dust bags.

The 3-pack dust-bag page says those bags are made specifically for L20 Ultra and L10s Ultra. It does not name L10s Ultra Gen 2. A shared bag with L20 Ultra does not mean the L20 kit is this kit.

The dust-box filter title names L10s Ultra, L10s Pro Gen2, L10s Plus, and D10s Plus Gen2. The product text also names GoVac 300, GoVac 200, L10s Pro, and D10s Plus. L10s Ultra Gen 2 and L10s Pro Ultra are not in that title.

Gen 2 is listed on another Dreame kit. That kit’s title leads with L40s Ultra AE, L40s Ultra CE, and L10s Ultra Gen2, and its contents are 1 main brush, 2 side brushes, 2 dust-collection bags, 2 dust-box filters, and 3 wash-free mop pads. This original kit uses 6 mop pads, not those 3 wash-free pads.
