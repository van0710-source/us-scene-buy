What changes the fit on the Roborock Qrevo Pro

On the robot or in the app, the name is Roborock Qrevo Pro. Roborock’s Pro page says that during a transitional period the same machine may be depicted as Roborock Q Revo Pro.

Qrevo S Pro has its own Roborock page. This fit check does not cover S Pro. The main-brush product title that includes the words Qrevo Pro does not include the words Qrevo S Pro.

Pro’s washable filter is the 2-piece whose title names Qrevo S, Qrevo MaxV, Qrevo Pro, Qrevo Plus, Qrevo Curv Series, Qrevo Edge Series, QV Series, and Qrevo S5V. A different Roborock filter is titled for original Qrevo only and says it is incompatible with other models in the Qrevo series.

The brush list is narrower than the filter list. Curv and Edge are on that washable-filter title, and Qrevo CurvX is on the 4-piece mop-cloth title, but the main-brush title that includes Pro does not name Qrevo Curv or Qrevo Edge. The Pro page lists an all-rubber main brush.

The 6-piece dock bag is titled for the Qrevo Series as a group. The same title also names S8 MaxV Ultra, S8 Max Ultra, Qrevo Curv Series, Qrevo Edge Series, Saros Series, and QV Series. A bag from that title does not by itself identify the Pro roller.
