Qrevo MaxV is the Dock 2.0 machine with an all-rubber brush and lifting dual mops. It is not S8 MaxV Ultra, even when a dust-bag title prints both names.

Easy mix-up: S8 MaxV Ultra sits on the same 6-piece dust-bag title as the Qrevo Series. The main-brush title names Qrevo MaxV and S7 MaxV, and it does not name S8 MaxV Ultra.

What changes the fit on the Roborock Qrevo MaxV

Confirm the label says Qrevo MaxV, also written Q Revo MaxV. The MaxV product page describes Multifunctional Dock 2.0, an all-rubber main brush, and dual spinning mops that lift. That page is not the S8 MaxV Ultra.

The MaxV mix-up is bag versus brush. The 6-piece dust-bag title puts “Qrevo Series” in the same product name as S8 MaxV Ultra and S8 Max Ultra. The main-brush title names Qrevo MaxV and also S7 MaxV, Qrevo, Qrevo S, Qrevo Pro, Qrevo Plus, QV 35A, QV 35S, Q7 Max, S7 Pro Ultra, and S7 Max Ultra. S8 MaxV Ultra is not in that brush title.

Qrevo Curv and Qrevo Edge are also absent from the MaxV main-brush title. They do show up on the 2-piece washable filter that names Qrevo MaxV with S, Pro, Plus, Curv Series, Edge Series, QV Series, and S5V. Order the filter and the brush from those two different titles.

MaxV mop cloths are the 4-piece whose title also names S5V, original Qrevo, Pro, S, Master, Plus, Slim, and CurvX. Master and Slim are on that cloth title, then leave the brush title. Roborock sells their washable filter as a separate 2-piece for Qrevo Master, Qrevo Slim, and Saros Z70.

Do not substitute the filter titled for original Qrevo only. That product says it is incompatible with other models in the Qrevo series.
