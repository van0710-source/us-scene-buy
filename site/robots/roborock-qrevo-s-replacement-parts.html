Easy mix-up: The main-brush title includes the words Qrevo S and does not include Qrevo S Pro. Curv and Edge are on the S washable-filter title, and they are not on the S brush-cover title.

What changes the fit on the Roborock Qrevo S

The label has to say Qrevo S, also written Q Revo S. Roborock publishes a separate Qrevo S Pro page. The main-brush product that lists Qrevo S does not list Qrevo S Pro.

S is one of the few models printed on the main-brush cover. That cover title is Qrevo, Qrevo Pro, Qrevo Plus, Qrevo S, Qrevo MaxV, QV 35A, and QV 35S. Qrevo Curv and Qrevo Edge are not on it.

The S washable filter is the 2-piece titled for Qrevo S, Qrevo MaxV, Qrevo Pro, Qrevo Plus, Qrevo Curv Series, Qrevo Edge Series, QV Series, and Qrevo S5V. The original-Qrevo-only filter says it is incompatible with other Qrevo-series models, so it is the wrong filter for S.

The mop-cloth title that includes Qrevo S also includes Qrevo Master, Qrevo Slim, and Qrevo CurvX. Those three names are not on the S main-brush title. Master and Slim use the washable filter sold for Qrevo Master, Qrevo Slim, and Saros Z70.

Dock bags for S are the 6-piece titled for the Qrevo Series. That title also names S8 MaxV Ultra, S8 Max Ultra, Curv Series, Edge Series, Saros Series, and QV Series. The bag title does not print the letter S by itself.
