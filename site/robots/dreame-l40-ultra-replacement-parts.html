What changes the fit on the Dreame L40 Ultra

Match a label that says L40 Ultra and does not say Gen 2. Dreame’s kit title for this machine is X40 Ultra/L40 Ultra. The compatibility line is X40 Ultra, L40 Ultra, and GoVac 508.

That kit contains 1 main brush, 2 side brushes, 2 dust-collection bags, 2 dust-box filters, and 6 wash-free mop pads.

The Gen 2 kit is a different product. Its title is L40 Ultra Gen 2, its compatibility line is L40 Ultra Gen 2 and GoVac 405, and its contents are 1 rubber roller brush, 2 side brushes, 2 dust-box filters, 2 dust bags, and 6 mops.

L40s Ultra AE and L40s Ultra CE look close and belong to the kit that leads with those names plus L10s Ultra Gen2. That kit also names X30 Ultra, D30 Ultra, GoVac 400, and GoVac 500, and it packs 3 wash-free mop pads rather than 6.
