Privacy

Privacy and affiliate disclosure

The current site is a static decision tool. It does not ask for an account, email address, payment information, or precise location.

Model selections and archived quiz answers

The robot-model selector sends you directly to a static compatibility page. It does not ask for an account or store a device serial number. The selected destination may be counted as an aggregate Umami event.

Archived kitchen-drain quiz answers are processed in your browser to display a recommendation. The current site does not send or store those answers on a US Scene Buy server.

Server and platform data

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most hosting providers, GitHub may process technical request data needed to deliver and secure the site. Refer to GitHub’s own privacy statement for its practices.

Amazon links

Some links are Amazon Associates “Special Links.” When you follow one, Amazon may use cookies or similar technologies to attribute a qualifying purchase and operate its services. Amazon controls its checkout, account, payment, shipping, and privacy practices.

Disclosure: As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.

Analytics

US Scene Buy uses a lightweight browser script (`assets/site.js`) to count same-day visits in local storage and record outbound Amazon link clicks on this device. That local data does not leave your browser unless you clear it.

When enabled, we also use Umami (cookieless page analytics) to measure aggregate visits, including campaign tags in links you followed (for example utm_source). Umami runs on Umami’s infrastructure; see their privacy policy for how they process requests.

You can clear local counters by removing site data for this domain in your browser.

This notice may be updated when the site’s data practices change. Last updated: September 14, 2026.
