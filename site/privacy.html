Privacy

Privacy and affiliate disclosure

The current site is a static decision tool. It does not ask for an account, email address, payment information, or precise location.

Quiz answers

Kitchen-drain quiz answers are processed in your browser to display a recommendation. The current site does not send or store those answers on a US Scene Buy server.

Server and platform data

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most hosting providers, GitHub may process technical request data needed to deliver and secure the site. Refer to GitHub’s own privacy statement for its practices.

Amazon links

Some links are Amazon Associates “Special Links.” When you follow one, Amazon may use cookies or similar technologies to attribute a qualifying purchase and operate its services. Amazon controls its checkout, account, payment, shipping, and privacy practices.

Disclosure: As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.

Analytics

US Scene Buy uses a lightweight browser script (`assets/site.js`) to count same-day visits in local storage and record outbound Amazon link clicks on this device. It does not send data to a US Scene Buy server.

You can clear this by removing site data for this domain in your browser.

This notice may be updated when the site’s data practices change. Last updated: August 17, 2026.
