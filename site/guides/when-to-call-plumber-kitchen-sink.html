When to call a plumber (kitchen sink)

Buying another bottle is not always cheaper than stopping. These are the stop-shopping signals.

  • Call soon: more than one drain backing up; sewage smell house-wide; water coming up elsewhere.
  • Call after DIY: plunger/snake failed; a chemical already went down and nothing moved.
  • Call instead of gel: septic + total clog, very old pipes, or landlord rules against harsh openers.
  • While waiting: don’t pour more product.
Permission not to buy is part of a good decision — especially when the line needs a machine, not a bottle.
