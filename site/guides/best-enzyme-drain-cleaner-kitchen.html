Enzyme drain cleaner for kitchen grease — when it actually fits

Enzymes are often positioned for organics, odor, and maintenance — not as a guaranteed “clear it in 15 minutes” emergency gel. Match the job before you buy.

  • Better fit: Slow drain after cooking, mild odor, septic or cautious households, maintenance after a fix.
  • Weak fit: Totally stuck sink with standing water — physical tools (or a labeled gel only if constraints allow) usually come first.
  • Fact check: Read this bottle’s claims (grease / septic / disposal). Brand marketing isn’t a timed clear guarantee.
  • Already used a chemical? Don’t stack an enzyme “to finish the job” on top of a harsh opener unless that label says it’s safe — usually you wait or call a pro.
We won’t promise a clear time. Decision rule: scenario + pipes + what you already tried → one next step.
