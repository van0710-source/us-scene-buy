When to escalate to a wet vac

Daily sealed hard floors with frequent spills/pets → a multi-surface wet vac can be worth it after you confirm the machine and solution are rated for your floor. That’s an upgrade path, not the first click for every mess.

Steam + some vinyl/laminate finishes = warping risk. If the floor maker says no steam / no soaking, believe them over a viral demo.
