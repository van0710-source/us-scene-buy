City sewer vs septic — why the same bottle isn’t the default

Our quiz asks where the drain goes because that single answer flips how aggressive a product recommendation should be.

  • Septic (or unsure): Don’t lead with “industrial strength.” Physical tools + label-checked enzyme/maintenance paths first; full clogs often mean plumber over panic gel.
  • City sewer: A labeled clog gel can be on the table when there’s a hard stoppage, no rental/old-pipe/kids flags, and nothing chemical already poured — plunger/snake still allowed first.
  • Either system: Never mix drain chemicals. No guaranteed clear time. Buying nothing / waiting can be correct.
This is decision help, not a plumbing inspection. Whole-house backups → pro, not more DIY bottles.
