Baking soda + vinegar for kitchen drains

Popular, cheap, and often oversold. Foam is not the same as clearing a grease plug.

  • May help: mild odor / light residue for some households.
  • Won’t reliably fix: standing water, heavy grease after cooking, or a hard stoppage.
  • Don’t combine with commercial drain openers — chemistry stacking is a safety risk.
  • Better decision path: physical first if stuck; constrained product pick if buying; wait/pro if already poured harsh chemistry.
