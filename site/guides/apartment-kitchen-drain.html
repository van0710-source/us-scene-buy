Apartment kitchen clog? Keep the lease (and your lungs) in mind.

Harsh drain openers can conflict with building rules — and stacking chemicals is never worth it. Here’s a calmer path.

If you rent, “industrial strength” is rarely the first move. Landlords may restrict chemicals; older buildings often have older pipes. Start with mechanical options or maintenance-leaning products, and call maintenance when it’s a full backup.

  • Check whether your lease or building bans chemical openers.
  • If you already poured one product, don’t buy a second to stack.
  • Fully stuck across fixtures → stop DIY and notify the landlord/plumber.
Never mix drain chemicals. When in doubt, wait for building maintenance.
