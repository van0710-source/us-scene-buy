Clean-first checklist

  1. Power off the disposal at the switch or breaker before scrubbing accessible areas.
  2. Scrub the splash guard top and underside—film hides there and smells when water runs.
  3. Run cold water + ice + dish soap only if your disposal manual allows it.
  4. Check timing: odor only when the dishwasher runs may point to hose or trap issues, not a clog gel.
  • Then consider: An enzyme cleaner marketed for organics/odor — not a guarantee, check the bottle for disposal use.
  • Avoid: Pouring a harsh clog gel “just in case” when nothing is blocked — extra chemistry without a blockage to clear.
  • Septic / rental / kids: Stay on clean + enzyme / wait; skip harsh openers as the default.
Standing sewage in other fixtures, or a sudden full stoppage → stop DIY shopping and call a plumber/landlord.
