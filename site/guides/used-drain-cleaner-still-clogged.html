Kitchen sink still clogged after Drano or another opener?

The next bottle is usually the wrong move. Chemistry already in the line changes what’s safe.

  • Do not stack: A second gel/enzyme “to finish the job” can splash, react, or make a plumber visit harder.
  • Read the first label: Wait times, ventilation, and “do not plunge” warnings matter more than forum tips.
  • If still stuck: Call a plumber (or landlord if you rent) rather than shopping another opener.
  • If flow returns later: Shift to habits + maintenance — not another emergency gel by default.
Never mix drain chemicals. No guaranteed clear time. Permission not to buy is a valid outcome.
