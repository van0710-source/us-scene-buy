Our compatibility method

Exact model first. Sources left visible.

US Scene Buy helps shoppers avoid look-alike replacement parts for robot vacuums. It is not a testing laboratory, repair service, manufacturer, or retailer.

How a fit page is made

  1. Normalize the full model name: every suffix stays visible, including Pro, MaxV, Plus, Ultra, or Gen 2.
  2. Group only documented families: bags, filters, mop pads, and brushes are matched from manufacturer compatibility lists or model pages.
  3. Show the easy mix-up: each page names a nearby model or generation that should not be assumed compatible.
  4. Separate evidence from shopping: manufacturer pages support the fit note; Amazon links show current shopping options.

What a compatibility page does not mean

  • We do not claim first-hand durability, filtration, or cleaning-performance testing.
  • A search result is not automatically compatible; verify that the current listing names the exact model.
  • We do not recommend batteries, internal repairs, motors, or safety-critical modifications.
  • Model lists, sellers, stock, and prices can change; re-check both the manufacturer source and Amazon listing.
